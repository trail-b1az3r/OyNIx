Tabs

Choose a layout in Settings › Tabs:

  • Standard: a tab bar under the toolbar.
  • Compact: tabs inside the toolbar, for small screens.
  • Vertical: a list in the sidebar, for people who keep forty tabs open.

Drag tabs to reorder them, or drag one out to a new window. Right-click a tab to pin, mute, duplicate or close the ones around it. CtrlShiftT brings back the last one you closed.

Tabs you haven’t looked at for 30 minutes are unloaded to free memory and reload when you switch to them. You can change the time, or turn this off, under Memory. Low Power Mode goes further: it freezes background tabs and turns off animations.

The address field

When you’re not typing, the field shows just the site’s name. Click it to see the full address.

As you type, OyNIx completes the address inline from your history and bookmarks. goo becomes google.com, and suggestions are shown the way you’d write them, without https://www. or a trailing slash. Press Enter to accept, or Delete to drop the completion.

Anything that isn’t an address is a search. Start with a !bang to search a particular site:

TypeSearches
!wWikipedia
!ghGitHub
!ytYouTube
!mdnMDN Web Docs
!awArchWiki

The full list is under Settings › Search. Tracking parameters such as utm_source and fbclid are removed from addresses you type or paste.

History

CtrlH opens the history page, grouped by day and searchable. Click an entry to open it, middle-click for a new tab, or use the × to remove an entry or every visit to that site. Clear Browsing Data removes history, cookies and the cache for the last hour, day or week, or for all time.

Right-click (or press and hold) the Back or Forward button to jump several steps at once.

Reading

  • Reader View (CtrlShiftR): an icon appears in the address field on pages with an article. It shows the text and pictures in a clean column.
  • Reading List (CtrlShiftD): save now, read later. Unread pages show on the Start Page and in the sidebar.
  • Focus Mode (CtrlShiftF): hides the toolbar and tabs. Point at the top edge to get them back.

Appearance

Settings › Appearance sets the theme (light, dark or match the system) and the accent colour every other colour is made from.

Website appearance decides what pages see:

  • Match OyNIx: dark pages when OyNIx is dark. Sites with their own dark theme use it; others are darkened automatically.
  • Always dark or Always light: regardless of the OyNIx theme.

If a darkened site looks wrong, click the site’s icon at the left of the address field and turn off Dark mode for that site.

Open settings in chooses between a tab (the default) and a separate window. See Hyprland and tiling window managers if you use the window.

Downloads

Downloads go to your Downloads folder. CtrlJ shows them, with pause, resume, open and show-in-folder. Turn on Ask where to save each file under Settings › General to pick a place every time.
